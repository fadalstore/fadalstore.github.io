## Verified facts and editorial use

### Kindle Paperwhite (2024 / 12th Gen)

PCMag lists it as best Kindle overall with a 7-inch display, speed, waterproofing, and a 4.5/5 score. Amazon’s official page lists a 7-inch glare-free 300 ppi display, adjustable warm light, up to 12 weeks of battery life, IPX8 waterproofing, and a 16GB model at $199.99 at the time of access. Prices can change by market and promotion.

### Kindle (2024)

Wirecutter lists the 2024 base Kindle as its top pick for most people, citing portability, 6-inch screen, USB-C, 16GB storage and access to Amazon’s ebook ecosystem. Amazon’s official page lists a 6-inch glare-free 300 ppi display, up to 6 weeks of battery life, and a $149.99 list price at the time of access.

### Kobo Clara Colour

PCMag lists the Kobo Clara Colour as a strong library-oriented option. Kobo’s official page lists a 6-inch E Ink Kaleido 3 display, 300 ppi black-and-white / 150 ppi color, 16GB storage, IPX8 water resistance, ComfortLight PRO, Libby/OverDrive availability in select countries, audiobook Bluetooth in select countries, repairability, and up to 42 days under stated test conditions. Official price shown: $179.99 at access time.

### Kobo Libra Colour

Kobo’s official page lists a 7-inch E Ink Kaleido 3 display, 32GB storage, 300 ppi black-and-white / 150 ppi color, IPX8 protection, page-turn buttons, Kobo Stylus 2 compatibility, Dropbox/Google Drive notebook support, Libby/OverDrive in select countries, and up to 40 days under stated test conditions. Official price shown: $259.99 at access time.

### BOOX Go Color 7 (Gen II)

PCMag lists the model as a color ereader/notebook hybrid with third-party app access, note-taking and page-turn buttons, while warning that it is not waterproof. BOOX’s official page lists a 7-inch Kaleido 3 display, 300 ppi black-and-white / 150 ppi color, Android 13, Google Play Store, 4GB RAM, 64GB storage plus microSD, 2,300mAh battery, stylus support, and a water-repellent—not waterproof—design. Official price shown: $269.99 at access time.

## Editorial conclusions for Fadal Store

- Best overall for most readers: Kindle Paperwhite, because it balances display size, speed, warm light, waterproofing and store ecosystem.
- Best budget: Kindle 2024, if the reader wants the smallest and least expensive dedicated reader and does not need waterproofing or warm light.
- Best for library users / non-Amazon ecosystem: Kobo Clara BW or Clara Colour, depending on whether color matters. Library availability varies by country.
- Best color reader for annotations: Kobo Libra Colour, if page-turn buttons, color markups and stylus-compatible notebooks are priorities.
- Best for apps, formats and hybrid reading: BOOX Go Color 7 Gen II, but it is more complex and should not be marketed as waterproof.
- Best for children: Kindle Paperwhite Kids, based on PCMag’s pick, with case, Kids+ trial and replacement warranty; verify current local availability and pricing.
