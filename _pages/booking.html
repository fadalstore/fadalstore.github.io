Ballan Qabasho (Appointment Booking)

Dooro waqtiga kugu habboon si aad u hesho la tashi ku saabsan ganacsiga online-ka.

Maxaad uga baahan tahay ballan?

  • La tashi ku saabsan sida lacag looga sameeyo internet-ka.
  • Caawinaad ku saabsan dhismaha website-yada.
  • Hagaajinta xirfadahaaga dhijitaalka ah.

Haddii aad dhib kala kulanto booking-ka, fadlan nagala soo xiriir halkan.
