# Siyaasadda Tafatirka iyo Kalsoonida

Fadal Store waa mareeg madax-bannaan oo Somali iyo English ah. Waxaan daabacnaa waxbarasho, tignoolajiyad, shaqo online ah, buugaag, reviews, iyo qisooyin. Boggan wuxuu sharxayaa sida aan u ilaalinno hufnaanta iyo kalsoonida akhristaha.

## Waxa aan ballanqaadno

- Waxaan kala saarno **xaqiiqo, aragti, talo, iyo mala-awaal**.
- Waxaan ku darnaa ilo ama links rasmi ah marka maqaalka uu ku tiirsan yahay xaqiiqo la hubin karo.
- Ma ballanqaadno dakhli, shaqo, maalgelin, ama natiijo gaar ah.
- Waxaan saxnaa khaladaadka la xaqiijiyo, waxaana cusboonaysiinnaa maqaalka marka xogtu isbeddelayso.
- Ma waydiisanno akhristayaasha password, lacag, ama xog xasaasi ah email ama social media aan la xaqiijin.

## Reviews iyo affiliate links

Qaar ka mid ah links-ku waa affiliate links. Haddii akhriste wax ka iibsado affiliate link, Fadal Store wuxuu heli karaa commission aan kharash dheeraad ah ku iman akhristaha. Affiliate relationship-ku ma dammaanad qaadayo in adeeg ama alaab ay ku habboon tahay qof kasta. Akhristuhu waa inuu hubiyaa qiimaha, shuruudaha, iyo amniga bogga rasmiga ah ka hor inta uusan bixin lacag ama xog.

## Saxitaan iyo cabasho

Haddii aad aragto khalad, link jaban, xog aan sax ahayn, ama arrin xuquuqda la xiriirta, email u dir **fadalstore7@gmail.com** adigoo ku daraya URL-ka maqaalka iyo faahfaahinta sixitaanka. Waxaan dib u eegnaa codsiyada si macquul ah, kadibna waxaan ku dari karnaa sixitaan ama qoraal cusboonaysiin ah.

# Editorial Policy and Trust Standards

Fadal Store is an independent Somali and English website covering education, technology, online work, books, reviews, and true stories. This page explains how we work to keep our content transparent and useful.

## Our commitments

- We distinguish **verified facts, opinion, advice, and speculation**.
- We link to official or recognized sources when an article depends on verifiable claims.
- We do not guarantee income, employment, investment returns, or specific results.
- We correct verified errors and update articles when important information changes.
- We never ask readers to send passwords, payments, or sensitive information through an unverified message.

## Reviews and affiliate links

Some links are affiliate links. If a reader purchases through one, Fadal Store may receive a commission at no additional cost to the reader. An affiliate relationship is not a guarantee that a product or service is suitable for everyone. Verify current prices, terms, and security on the official provider website before paying or sharing information.

## Corrections and complaints

For a correction, broken link, inaccurate claim, or rights-related concern, email **fadalstore7@gmail.com** with the article URL and details. We review requests reasonably and may add a correction or update note.

## Ownership and contact

Fadal Store is managed by **Fadal Yare**. See the [About page]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/) for more background and the [Contact page]({{ site.baseurl }}/contact.html) for official contact options.

## Security

Fadal Store does not store passwords or secrets in static website pages. If anyone claiming to represent Fadal Store asks for a password or payment through an unverified message, do not share it; contact the official email above.
