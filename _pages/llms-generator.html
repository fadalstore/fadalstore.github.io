LLMS.txt Generator

U beddel website-kaaga mid AI-ga u saaxiib ah (AI-friendly)

Hal link xariiq kasta.

Maxay tahay LLMS.txt?

Waa fayl cusub oo la mid ah robots.txt, laakiin loogu talagalay inuu AI-ga (sida Claude iyo ChatGPT) u sharxo xogta ugu muhiimsan ee website-kaaga.

Faa'iidooyinka:

  • AI-ga ayaa si sax ah u fahmaya nuxurkaaga.
  • Waxay fududaynaysaa in website-kaaga loo isticmaalo tixraac.
  • Waxay kordhinaysaa "AI Search Visibility" (SEO-ga cusub).

Natiijada:


        
        

            
 Tallaabada Xigta:

            
Faylkan geli root directory (folder-ka ugu weyn) ee website-kaaga. Hubi in link-gu yahay yourdomain.com/llms.txt.

        

    


    

        
Su'aalaha Badanaa La Isweydiiyo (FAQ)

        
        

            
Xaggee baan dhigayaa faylkan?

            
Faylka llms.txt waa in la dhigaa folder-ka ugu sareeya ee website-kaaga (public_html ama root). Haddii aad isticmaalayso GitHub Pages, ku rid folder-ka weyn.

        


        

            
Ma qasab baa inaan link-yada oo dhan ku daro?

            
Maya, ku dar kaliya link-yada ugu muhiimsan ee aad rabto in AI-gu akhriyo, sida bogga "About", "Services", ama "Documentation".

        


        

            
Ma beddelayaa robots.txt?

            
Maya, robots.txt wuxuu u sheegaa search engines-ka meesha ay geli karaan, halka llms.txt uu AI-ga u sharxayo waxa ku dhex jira website-ka.
