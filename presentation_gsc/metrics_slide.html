2. Falanqaynta Ere-baadhisaha (Queries & Impressions)

Halbeegga (Metric) Waa Maxay? Maxaa Laga Doonayaa?
Impressions Inta jeer ee website-kaaga la arkay natiijooyinka Google. In la kordhiyo iyadoo la qorayo maqaallo cusub.
Clicks Inta jeer ee la gujiyey link-ga website-kaaga. In la kordhiyo iyadoo la hagaajinayo cinwaannada.
Average Position Booska aad kaga jirto Google Search (1-100). In la gaaro bogga 1-aad (Top 3-10).
