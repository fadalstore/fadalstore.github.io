3. Xeeladda "Low-Hanging Fruit"

Waa Maxay Fursadaha Degdeg ah?

Waa bogagga website-kaaga ee Google ay u aqoonsatay inay muhiim yihiin laakiin ku jira Booska 5-aad ilaa 15-aad.

Bogaggan uma baahna in dib loo qoro, ee waxay u baahan yihiin oo kaliya "Hagaajin Yar" si ay u soo galaan 3-da boos ee ugu sarreeya.

1

Cusboonaysiinta Nuxurka

Ku dar faahfaahin dheeraad ah, su'aalo (FAQs), iyo xog cusub oo maqaalka ka dhigaysa mid dhamaystiran.

2

Hagaajinta Title-ka

Ka dhig cinwaanka mid soo jiidasho leh oo dadka ku dhiirigelinaya inay gujiyaan (CTR Optimization).

3

Xiriirinta Gudaha

Maqaallada kale ee website-ka ku jira ka soo dir link-yo (Internal Links) ku fiiqaya boggan si uu Google ugu xoogaysto.
