<h2 id="ending">The Sequel Ending Explained: The Final Confrontation</h2>
<p>As Anna investigates the person sending her excerpts of the novel <em>The Afterword</em>, she discovers the sender is <strong>Leo</strong>, who was the boyfriend of her late brother, Evan Parker. Leo knows everything—about Evan's death, Jake's "suicide," and Anna's true identity.</p>

<p>The climax occurs when Anna realizes that Leo isn't just looking for money; he wants justice for Evan. However, Anna, ever the master storyteller and manipulator, manages to lure Leo into a trap. In a chilling conclusion, Anna ensures that Leo can no longer threaten her, effectively "editing" him out of her life just as she did with Jake. She ends the book as the sole owner of her narrative, once again proving that in the world of Jean Hanff Korelitz, the most ruthless writer wins.</p>
