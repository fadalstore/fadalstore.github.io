<h2 id="release">Chaosborn Book 3 Expected Release Date</h2>
<p>As of August 2026, <strong>Madeleine Eliot</strong> has not officially announced a release date for the third and final book in the <em>Chaosborn</em> trilogy. However, we can make a strong prediction based on her previous publishing schedule.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Heart of Chaos (Book 1):</strong> Released May 2025</li>
  <li><strong>Soul of Fire (Book 2):</strong> Released August 1, 2026</li>
</ul>

<p>Given the 15-month gap between the first two books, we expect <strong>Chaosborn Book 3 to be released in late 2027 or early 2028</strong>. The author has mentioned on Instagram that she is currently in the early drafting stages, and she aims to give the trilogy the epic conclusion it deserves.</p>
