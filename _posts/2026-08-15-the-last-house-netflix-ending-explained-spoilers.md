<h2 id="plot">The Plot: Trapped in a Nightmare</h2>
<p>Netflix's latest sci-fi hit, <strong>The Last House</strong>, follows the Delgado family (played by <strong>Greta Lee</strong> and <strong>Wagner Moura</strong>) who wake up to find their home sealed by an invisible, impenetrable force. Outside, a mysterious and deadly rain is falling, and anyone caught in it is instantly hunted by nearly invisible, aquatic creatures.</p>

<p>The tension builds as the family realizes they aren't just trapped—they are being watched. As the house begins to flood from the relentless rain, the survival stakes reach a breaking point.</p>

<h2 id="monsters">What Are the Rain Monsters?</h2>
<p>One of the biggest questions in <em>The Last House</em> is the identity of the creatures. The film reveals that these "Rain Monsters" are ancient, aquatic beings that travel through water. They aren't necessarily aliens, but rather a primordial force reclaimed by the Earth as the sea levels rise. They can move through the rain itself, making them almost impossible to see until they strike.</p>

<h2 id="ending">The Last House Ending Explained: Why Did They Let Them Go?</h2>
<p>The climax of the film occurs in the flooded attic. After a desperate struggle, the family manages to trap one of the smaller creatures. Instead of killing it, the young daughter, Sofia, convinces her parents to let it go. This act of mercy is the turning point of the movie.</p>

<h3>The Twist of Mercy</h3>
<p>In the final scene, as the larger monsters surround the house, the creature they spared communicates with the others. In a shocking twist, the monsters retreat, sparing the Delgado family while every other house in the neighborhood is decimated. The ending suggests that the creatures possess a high level of intelligence and a moral code of reciprocity.</p>

<h3>Who Survives?</h3>
<p>The entire Delgado family survives the ordeal. As the rain finally stops and the invisible barrier vanishes, they step outside to a world that has been completely transformed. The "Last House" stands alone in a silent, water-logged landscape.</p>

<h2 id="review">Review: A New Sci-Fi Classic?</h2>
<p>With a <strong>4.7/5 rating</strong>, <em>The Last House</em> is being compared to <em>A Quiet Place</em> and <em>Bird Box</em>. The cinematography during the flood scenes is breathtaking, and the emotional core of the family's bond makes the survival horror feel deeply personal. It is undoubtedly one of the best Netflix originals of 2026.</p>
