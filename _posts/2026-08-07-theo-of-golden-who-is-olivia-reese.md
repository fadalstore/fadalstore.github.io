## Jawaabta Kooban

Olivia Reese waa magaca la siiyay Willa Francesca markii la korsaday. Waxay ku soo baxdaa buugga si lama filaan ah, iyadoo xafiiska James Ponder timid warqad ay sitaa — warqad Theo horeba u soo diray.

## Sidee Ay Ugu Timid Golden?

- Kadib dhimashadii Theo, Olivia Reese ayaa si lama filaan ah ugu timid xafiiska James Ponder

- Ponder ayaa markii dambe garwaaqsaday inay tahay Willa — gabadhii Ellen ee la waayay

## Xiriirka Dhexmara Olivia iyo Ellen

Olivia Reese (Willa) waa dhalaankii Ellen, oo laga qaaday isla mar ahaantay dhalatay, taasoo dhacday kadib markii gacaliyaha Ellen la dilay iyo markii maamulka ay ku qiimeeyeen inaanay ku filnayn korinta ilmo. Ellen weligeed uma tagin xasuustaas, waxayna hayn jirtay qanjeef leh tin ilmaheeda.

## Doorka Olivia/Willa Sheekada Guud
