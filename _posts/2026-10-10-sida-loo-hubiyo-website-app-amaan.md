Internet-ka waxaa ku jira websites iyo apps faa'iido leh, laakiin sidoo kale waxaa jira kuwo dadka ku khiyaaneeya xog, lacag, ama akoonno. **HTTPS ama calaamadda qufulka keliya ma caddeeyaan in website-ku yahay mid la aamini karo.** Isticmaal liiskan hubinta ka hor inta aadan wax tallaabo ah qaadin.

## 1. Hubi magaca domain-ka

Akhri domain-ka si taxaddar leh. Khiyaanadu waxay mararka qaar isticmaashaa xarfo isku eg, magac dheer oo aan caadi ahayn, ama domain u eg shirkad caan ah. Haddii link laguu soo diray, si toos ah u qor cinwaanka website-ka ama ka raadi magaca rasmiga ah halkii aad gujin lahayd link aan la hubin.

Domain cusub kaligiis ma caddaynayo khiyaano, laakiin waa sabab aad u sameyso baaritaan dheeraad ah. Hubi haddii website-ku leeyahay cinwaan cad, email rasmi ah, iyo macluumaad lala xiriiri karo.

## 2. Akhri bogagga kalsoonida iyo siyaasadaha

Website xirfad leh waa inuu si cad u muujiyo:

- cidda maamusha ama qorta content-ka;
- bogga xiriirka iyo email shaqaynaya;
- Privacy Policy iyo Terms of Service;
- sida affiliate links, xayeysiisyada, ama reviews loo maareeyo;
- habka lagu soo sheego khalad ama arrin amni.

Haddii boggu sameeyo reviews, raadi **Editorial Policy** ama Trust Standards si aad u ogaato sida macluumaadka loo baaro iyo sida khaladaadka loo saxo. Fadal Store wuxuu ku sharaxaa arrimahan bogga [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/) iyo [About](/about/).

## 3. Ha ku degdegin lacag-bixin ama xog xasaasi ah

Ha gelin password, lambarka kaarka, PIN, ama sawirka aqoonsiga website aadan si madax-bannaan u xaqiijin. Haddii website-ku ku cadaadiyo “bixi hadda”, “abaalmarinta waa kaa dhammaanaysaa”, ama “account-kaaga waa la xirayaa”, jooji oo baar marka hore.

Adeeg sharci ah waa inuu sharxaa qiimaha, lacag-celinta, iyo cidda lacagta qaadanaysa. Haddii aad wax iibsanayso, isticmaal hab lacag-bixin bixiya ilaalin iyo receipt aad kaydsan karto.

## 4. Eeg tayada iyo joogteynta content-ka

Qoraallo badan oo leh cinwaanno buunbuunin ah, ballanqaadyo aan macquul ahayn, khaladaad badan, ama xog aan lahayn taariikh iyo il waxay u baahan yihiin taxaddar. Ha ku tiirsanaan hal review ama hal trust score oo keliya; isbarbar dhig ilo badan oo madax-bannaan.

Sidoo kale hubi in maqaalka uu kala saarayo xaqiiqo, aragti, iyo xayeysiis. Review daacad ahi waa inuu xusaa faa'iidooyinka iyo xaddidaadaha, ee uusan kaliya bixin ballanqaadyo waaweyn.

## 5. Samee baaritaan dheeraad ah

Ka raadi magaca shirkadda, app-ka, ama domain-ka erayo ay ka mid yihiin “complaint”, “privacy”, “refund”, ama “security”. Akhri ilo kala duwan, eeg taariikhda xogta, oo hubi in wararku ka yimaadaan ilo dhab ah.

Aaladaha domain reputation-ka waxay bixiyaan tilmaamo, laakiin natiijadoodu ma aha go'aan kama dambays ah. Trust score hoose wuxuu noqon karaa digniin muhiim ah, halka score sare uusan dammaanad qaadayn badbaado buuxda.

## Liis kooban oo aad xasuusato

1. Akhri domain-ka oo ha gujin link kasta.
2. Hubi About, Contact, Privacy Policy, iyo Terms.
3. Raadi qoraa iyo taariikh la fahmi karo.
4. Ha bixin xog ama lacag ilaa aad xaqiijiso.
5. Isbarbar dhig ilo madax-bannaan.
6. Haddii wax kuu muuqdaan degdeg ama aad u qurux badan, hakad geli.

> **Gunaanad:** Badbaadada internet-ku waxay ku dhisan tahay xaqiijin, ma aha calaamadda qufulka oo keliya. Website ama app wanaagsan wuxuu si cad u sheegaa cidda ka dambaysa, sida uu u shaqeeyo, iyo sida uu u saxo khaladaadka.
