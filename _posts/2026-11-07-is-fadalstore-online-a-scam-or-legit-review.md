## Is it safe to buy an ebook from the website?

Use the same precautions you would use with any new digital store. Before paying, check the product description, format, delivery method, price, refund terms, and the identity of the payment provider. Save the receipt and do not send a password, card PIN, identity document, or one-time verification code by email or chat.

If a product page does not explain what you will receive, how delivery works, or how to request help, do not purchase until those details are clear. A legitimate seller should not pressure you with countdowns, threats, or promises of guaranteed income.

For a low-risk first test, begin with a product you understand, use a payment method that gives you a transaction record, and keep all communication. Never pay a second “release fee” or “verification fee” to receive a digital file unless you have independently confirmed the charge with the payment provider.

## How should readers judge the site?

A balanced review should consider both warning signs and positive evidence:

### Warning signs to take seriously

- The domain is young or has little independent history.
- A third-party service gives it a low reputation score.
- The site does not clearly identify its publisher or contact route.
- Product, refund, delivery, or privacy information is missing.
- The site pressures visitors to pay immediately.
- Reviews contain only praise and no limitations, sources, or dates.

### Evidence that can reduce uncertainty

- The site explains who writes and edits its content.
- It provides a working contact route and responds to corrections.
- Product pages clearly state what is included and how delivery works.
- Payment is handled through a recognizable provider with a receipt.
- Articles distinguish advertising, affiliate links, opinion, and verified facts.
- Independent readers can find the site over time and report consistent experiences.

No single item proves legitimacy. The safest decision comes from considering the complete picture and only sharing the minimum information needed for the transaction.
