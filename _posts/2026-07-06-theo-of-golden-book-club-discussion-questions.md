### Summary

**🗣️ Spark the Conversation:** *Theo of Golden* by Allen Levi is a book that begs to be discussed. From the mysterious "bestowals" to the shocking ending, there is so much to unpack. Here are 20 questions to help your book club dive deep into the heart of Golden, Georgia.

## Introduction to the Discussion

When Allen Levi wrote *Theo of Golden*, he created more than just a story; he created a meditation on kindness, grief, and the quiet ways we impact one another. Use these questions to guide your group through the many layers of this beautiful novel.

## General Impressions

1.  What was your initial impression of Theo when he first arrived in Golden? Did your opinion of him change as his secrets were revealed?
2.  Which of Theo’s "bestowals" (acts of kindness) touched you the most? Why?
3.  How would you describe the "vibe" of the town of Golden? Does it remind you of any place you’ve lived or visited?

## Character Deep Dives

4.  **Theo:** Why do you think Theo chose to live so simply in Golden despite his immense wealth and fame as an artist?
5.  **Ellen:** Ellen is a character who has lost so much. How does her friendship with Theo change her perspective on her own life?
6.  **Asher:** Discuss the revelation that Theo is Asher’s father. Why do you think Theo chose not to tell him while he was alive?
7.  **Simone:** What does Simone’s journey with her music represent in the larger context of the story?

## Themes and Symbols

8.  The novel features many portraits. What do these portraits symbolize about how we see—and fail to see—one another?
9.  Water and the town fountain are recurring images. What role does "cleansing" or "reflection" play in the story?
10. Discuss the theme of **Grief**. How do different characters (Theo, Ellen, Asher) handle their losses differently?
11. Kindness is often seen as a "weakness" in the modern world. How does this book challenge that idea?

## The Ending (Spoiler Alert!)

12. Were you surprised by the revelation of Theo’s true identity as the artist Zila? Did you pick up on any clues earlier in the book?
13. How did you feel about the way Theo died? Was it a fitting end for his character, or did it feel too tragic?
14. What did you think of the reunion between Ellen and her daughter Willa (Olivia Reese)? Was it the ending you were hoping for?
15. The attackers who caused Theo’s fall are never caught. Why do you think the author chose to leave that thread unresolved?

## Personal Reflection

16. If you had Theo’s resources, what "bestowal" would you make in your own community?
17. Has this book changed the way you look at the "strangers" in your own life?
18. Which character did you identify with the most, and why?
19. If you could ask Allen Levi one question about the book, what would it be?
20. In one word, how would you describe the legacy Theo left behind in Golden?

## Conclusion

*Theo of Golden* reminds us that our lives are intertwined in ways we rarely understand. We hope these questions help your book club appreciate the craftsmanship and heart that Allen Levi poured into this novel.
