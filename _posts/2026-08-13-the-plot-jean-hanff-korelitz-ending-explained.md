<h2 id="ending">The Plot Ending Explained: The Final Twist</h2>
<p>The biggest twist in <em>The Plot</em> is the identity of Jake's wife, <strong>Anna Williams</strong>. It turns out Anna is actually <strong>Diana Parker</strong>, the sister of the student Evan Parker. She is the person who lived the horrific true story that Jake stole for his book. She killed her brother to keep the secret, married Jake to reclaim her story, and ultimately poisons him to inherit his wealth and fame.</p>
