<h2 id="hosting">Step 1: Free Hosting with GitHub Pages</h2>
<p>GitHub Pages is the clear winner for free hosting. It's fast, secure, and Google loves it. Since it serves static files from a global CDN, my website consistently loads in under 1.5 seconds.</p>

<h3>Why GitHub Pages?</h3>
<ul>
    <li><strong>Speed:</strong> Blazing fast loading times (crucial for SEO).</li>
    <li><strong>Security:</strong> Free SSL certificate (HTTPS) included.</li>
    <li><strong>Reliability:</strong> 99.9% uptime powered by GitHub's infrastructure.</li>
</ul>
