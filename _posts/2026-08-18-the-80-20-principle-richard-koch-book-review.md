<p>If you feel overwhelmed by your to-do list, <strong>The 80/20 Principle</strong> by Richard Koch is the cure. Koch argues that the world is inherently "unbalanced"—most of what we do produces very little, while a tiny fraction of our actions produces almost everything that matters.</p>

<h2 id="key-takeaways">Key Takeaways from the Book</h2>
<ul>
    <li><strong>The 80/20 Rule in Business:</strong> Discover how 20% of your products or customers likely generate 80% of your profits.</li>
    <li><strong>The 80/20 Rule in Life:</strong> Identify the 20% of your friends and activities that provide 80% of your happiness.</li>
    <li><strong>Focus on Assets:</strong> Instead of trying to fix your weaknesses, double down on your 20% of strengths.</li>
</ul>
