Maqaal cusub oo la daabacay ma soo muuqdo Google isla markiiba. Google wuxuu u baahan yahay inuu ogaado URL-ka, booqdo bogga, qiimeeyo tayada, kadibna go'aansado in uu ku daro natiijooyinka raadinta. **Sitemap iyo Search Console waxay fududeeyaan helitaanka, laakiin ma dammaanad qaadaan indexing degdeg ah.**

## Waa maxay sitemap?

Sitemap waa fayl ku taxan URL-yada muhiimka ah ee website-ka. Wuxuu search engine-ka ka caawiyaa inuu fahmo bogagga aad rabto in la ogaado. Sitemap-ka waa inuu ka koobnaadaa URLs rasmi ah oo shaqaynaya, ee uusan ku darin bogag tijaabo, duplicate, ama bogag aad ku qortay `noindex`.

Tusaale ahaan, website-ku wuxuu sitemap-ka ku heli karaa:

```text
https://example.com/sitemap.xml
```

Ka hor inta aadan gudbin, browser-ka ku fur URL-kaas oo hubi in uu si caadi ah u soo baxayo.

## Tallaabada 1: Hubi waxyaabaha aasaasiga ah

Hubi in maqaalka cusub:

- leeyahay URL gaar ah oo joogto ah;
- uusan lahayn `noindex` haddii aad rabto in la raadiyo;
- leeyahay title iyo description qeexaya waxa ku jira;
- ku xiran yahay bog kale oo website-kaaga ah;
- ku jiro sitemap-ka;
- si fiican uga furmayo telefoon iyo desktop;
- aanu lahayn links jaban ama content placeholder ah.

Sidoo kale hubi in `robots.txt` uusan si kama' ah u xannibin maqaalka ama qaybta muhiimka ah ee website-ka.

## Tallaabada 2: Gudbi sitemap-ka

1. Gal Google Search Console.
2. Dooro property-ga saxda ah, tusaale `https://example.com/`.
3. Fur qaybta **Sitemaps**.
4. Geli `sitemap.xml` haddii domain-ka hore uga muuqdo sanduuqa.
5. Guji **Submit**.
6. Sug inta Google ka akhrinayo sitemap-ka.

Haddii status-ku noqdo “Success”, taasi waxay ka dhigan tahay in Google uu faylka akhriyey. Ma aha in URL kasta oo ku jira isla markiiba la index-gareeyay; xaaladda bogagga ka eeg qaybta **Pages**.

## Tallaabada 3: Isticmaal URL Inspection maqaalka muhiimka ah

Haddii aad leedahay maqaal muhiim ah oo cusub, ku geli URL-kiisa sanduuqa URL Inspection. Natiijadu waxay kuu sheegi kartaa in URL-ku hore u yaqaanno Google, haddii crawl la oggol yahay, iyo canonical-ka la doortay.

Haddii URL-ku diyaar yahay, dooro **Request Indexing**. Codsiyada noocan ahi waxay ku caawin karaan discovery, laakiin Google ayaa go'aamisa goorta iyo in bogga la index-gareeyo. Ha ku celcelin codsiga marar badan muddo gaaban.

## Tallaabada 4: Samee internal links

Maqaal cusub oo aan meel kale laga xiriirin wuxuu noqon karaa mid adag in la helo. Ku xiro maqaalka cusub:

- homepage-ka ama qaybta ku habboon;
- maqaal hore oo mowduuc la xiriira;
- category page;
- bogga About ama guide haddii uu macno samaynayo.

Links-ku waa inay caawiyaan akhristaha, ma aha in si aan dabiici ahayn loogu buuxiyo keywords.

## Haddii boggu weli uusan index-garayn

Sababaha caadiga ah waxaa ka mid ah content aad u gaaban, duplicate content, canonical khaldan, bog aan lahayn links gudaha ah, server error, ama Google oo aan weli helin fursad uu ku crawl-gareeyo. Hagaaji sababta, sii website-ka waqti, kadibna mar kale eeg URL Inspection.

Ha iibsan adeegyo kuu ballanqaadaya “100% Google indexing” ama links aan la fahmi karin. Adeegyada noocaas ahi waxay dhaawici karaan sumcadda website-ka halkii ay ka caawin lahaayeen.

> **Xusuusnow:** Sitemap-ku wuxuu Google tusayaa meesha ay bogagga muhiimka ahi yaallaan; content wanaagsan, website la isku halayn karo, iyo links dabiici ah ayaa ka caawiya Google inuu qiimeeyo bogaggaas.
